Aqua Sort — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

This policy covers the Aqua Sort Android app by VexlowHQ (package com.vexlowhq.watersort), separately from the general VexlowHQ website privacy policy.

Information we collect

Aqua Sort doesn't require you to register or create an account to play. We don't collect personal data directly, and the app has no login, chat or social features.

Your game progress (levels completed, stars, scores, achievements and their unlock dates) and your settings (language, sound, music, vibration, accessibility) are stored only on your device and are never sent to our servers. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them completely.

Advertising

We use Google AdMob to show ads. AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location derived from it, basic device information and ad interaction data in order to serve, measure and (where you allow it) personalize ads, and to prevent fraud.

  • Rewarded ads: shown only when you choose to watch one, in exchange for one extra empty bottle in the current level — never automatically.
  • Interstitial ads: shown occasionally between levels (never during gameplay or tutorials), at most once every few completed levels.
  • Consent: where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent through Google's consent tool before requesting personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy options.

This data is collected and processed by Google directly, per their own privacy policy and ads policy — we don't see or store it ourselves. Data is transmitted encrypted.

Children's privacy

Aqua Sort is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can reset or delete your device's advertising identifier, or opt out of personalized ads, from your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads). You can review your ad consent in the app at Settings → Privacy options (when available in your region), and delete all local game data at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We'll post any significant changes on this same page.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, email us at contact@vexlowhq.com.
