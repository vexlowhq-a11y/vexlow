How to play

Vex Dash is a single-button endless runner — tap, click, or press Space to jump over the spikes. Timing is everything, since the run only ends when you hit one. Along the way, hidden checkpoints let you collect the letters of VEXLOWHQ; get a letter once and it stays checked off across future runs, even if that specific run ends early.

Tips & strategy

  • Spike groups get denser and faster as your score climbs, so the run genuinely gets harder in real time, not just longer — don't get comfortable with the opening pace.
  • Letters spawn at fixed points along the run, not randomly. Miss one and you'll get another shot at it on your next attempt, since every run starts from the same beginning.
  • Short, early taps clear spikes more reliably than holding the button down — the jump arc is tuned for quick presses, not long holds.
