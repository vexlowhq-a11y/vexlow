🔤 Word Search

100 hand-picked levels across 4 difficulties — drag to find every word, unlock the next level, and collect achievements along the way.

How to play

Pick a level from the map — levels unlock as you complete the ones before them and collect enough stars. Click or tap a letter and drag in a straight line (horizontal, vertical, or diagonal, either direction) to select a word. A correct match locks in with its own color and gets struck off the list. Solve it fast and hint-free for the full 3 stars.

Tips & strategy

  • Scan for uncommon letters first (Q, X, Z, J) — they narrow down where a word could start much faster than scanning row by row.
  • Words can run in any of the 8 directions and can be found back-to-front, so don't rule out a match just because it reads backwards.
  • Stars are based on how long you take and how many hints you use — the faster and cleaner you solve it, the more stars you get.

Tech specs

  • TypeWord search puzzle, 100 levels
  • ControlsMouse drag or touch drag
  • DifficultiesEasy, Medium, Hard, Extreme
  • InterfaceEnglish, Spanish (word content currently in Spanish)
  • ScoringUp to 3 stars per level, plus unlockable achievements
