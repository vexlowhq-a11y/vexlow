How to play

A ball rolls forward on its own through a series of colored gates. Tap, or press Space, to cycle the ball's color through a fixed loop: red → blue → green → yellow → red. You can only pass through a gate if your color matches it at the exact moment you reach it — mismatch, and the run ends there.

Tips & strategy

  • Speed climbs continuously from the moment you start, with no early plateau — the biggest skill jump is getting comfortable tapping faster without losing track of your current color.
  • Because the color cycle is always in the same fixed order, you can count taps ahead of time for a gate that's still a few seconds out, instead of reacting at the last second.
  • Matching a gate is worth 10 points, and surviving longer at higher speed adds up steadily too — smooth, evenly-timed taps beat panicked last-second ones.
