What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that websites store in your browser to remember information about your visit.

Cookies we use

  • Essential: let the site function (e.g., remembering your light/dark mode preference).
  • Analytics: help us understand which content performs best, in aggregate.
  • Advertising: used by Google AdSense and other ad providers to show relevant advertising based on your interests.

Managing cookies

You can delete or block cookies from your browser settings. Note that blocking some cookies may affect how the site works. For Google's personalized advertising, you can adjust your preferences at adssettings.google.com.
