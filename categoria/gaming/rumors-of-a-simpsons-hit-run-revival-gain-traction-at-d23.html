Rumors of a Simpsons: Hit & Run Revival Gain Traction at D23

Matt Groening hinted at the return of the beloved video game during a recent event, fueling excitement among fans.

Leonardo Beltran·August 16, 2026·7 min

The world of video games has witnessed many classic titles that have attained cult status, but few have captured the hearts of players quite like The Simpsons: Hit & Run. Initially launched in 2003, the game became a defining experience for fans of both The Simpsons franchise and action-adventure gaming, blending an open-world environment with the show's signature humor and style. Now, whispers of a potential revival have begun to gain momentum, thanks to recent comments made by series creator Matt Groening.

At the D23 Expo, famed for showcasing Disney’s multitude of cultural offerings, Groening was notably asked about the possibility of a sequel or a remake for The Simpsons: Hit & Run. Groening's response hinted at exciting developments: "I think the original game is coming back in some form." This statement was followed by an enigmatic comment from current showrunner Matt Selman, saying "or not," creating an atmosphere filled with speculation and intrigue. The implications of Groening’s words suggest that there could indeed be plans in the pipeline, although the nature and specifics remain cloaked in uncertainty.

A Nostalgic Journey: The Original Game

The Simpsons: Hit & Run was widely praised for its immersive gameplay and witty writing, featuring many beloved characters from the long-running animated series. Players navigated a vibrant Springfield, completing various missions while enjoying a familiar sense of humor that only The Simpsons can deliver. Its unique blend of driving mechanics and adventure gameplay made it a standout title and a cherished experience for both casual gamers and die-hard fans alike. Its current relevance is underscored by the growing interest in remakes and sequels to nostalgic video games, as both gamers and industry professionals recognize the enduring appeal of these classic experiences.

Nostalgia Meets Modern Trends

Nostalgia in gaming has driven recent trends toward remasters, remakes, and sequels of beloved arcade and console titles. The success of remakes such as Tony Hawk's Pro Skater and the Crash Bandicoot N. Sane Trilogy indicates that there is a lucrative market for reintroducing classic games to both new players and returning fans. These revived titles often feature enhanced graphics and updated mechanics, which can help breathe new life into older franchises while preserving the core experiences that players loved.

Should The Simpsons: Hit & Run see a revival, it could tap into this lucrative resurgence, capturing a new generation of players while satisfying nostalgic fans craving a return to Springfield.

What’s Possible? The Future of Hit & Run

The response from Groening and Selman, while cryptic, could pave the way for various possibilities regarding a potential revival. Whether it entails a simple remaster with improved graphics, additional missions, or even an entire sequel with new gameplay mechanics remains to be seen. Regardless, the anticipation surrounding any project bearing the name of The Simpsons is sure to attract considerable excitement. The creators may leverage the current technological advancements and user preferences to craft an experience that resonates with the modern gaming landscape.

It is also essential to consider the broader implications this could have for The Simpsons franchise. A revival could not only reinvigorate interest in the original game but could also lead to new merchandise, collaborations, and even crossover opportunities with other franchises, reinforcing its status as a pop culture icon.

Fans Await More Details

As fans of The Simpsons and gamers alike eagerly await more concrete information, the excitement generated by Groening's hints at D23 serves as a reminder of the strong connection between television and gaming. This intersection continues to evolve, with both mediums influencing each other in unique ways. For now, while fans remain hopeful for further announcements regarding The Simpsons: Hit & Run, patience is essential as the creators work behind the scenes to make this revival a reality, if indeed it is in the works.
