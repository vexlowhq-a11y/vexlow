Netflix Sets October Theatrical, November Streaming Dates for 'Gentle Monster'

Léa Seydoux's Cannes drama, directed by Marie Kreutzer, opens in select U.S. theaters October 30 and arrives on Netflix November 18.

What Was Announced, and By Whom

Netflix has set release dates for "Gentle Monster," the Cannes Film Festival drama starring Léa Seydoux and directed by Marie Kreutzer: a limited U.S. theatrical release on October 30, 2026, followed by streaming in the U.S., Canada, U.K., Australia and New Zealand on November 18, 2026, according to Variety, with the November 18 streaming date independently confirmed by other outlets tracking the release.

The Story

The film follows Lucy (Seydoux), an experimental musician and mother, after her husband Philip (Laurence Rupp) is arrested on charges of possessing and distributing child sexual abuse material. Inspired by a real German criminal case, the story follows Lucy's shock, denial and search for the truth, alongside a parallel investigation storyline. The cast also includes Catherine Deneuve, Jella Haase and Sylvester Groth. The film carries an R rating for sexuality, graphic nudity and language, and marks Seydoux's first on-screen singing performance.

Where It Stands

"Gentle Monster" premiered at Cannes in May 2026 and is scheduled to screen at the Toronto International Film Festival in September 2026. It holds a 62% score on Rotten Tomatoes and has drawn early attention as a possible Best International Feature contender this awards season -- reception VexlowHQ has not independently verified beyond what review aggregators report, and which could shift as more critics weigh in.

Correcting the Record

The previous version of this article referred to the release as significant "as we look towards the end of 2023" -- nearly three years off from the film's actual 2026 release window, and never named the director, cast beyond Seydoux, or plot. This version replaces that error with the confirmed 2026 dates and adds the context the previous draft omitted.
