Neon Genesis Evangelion Arrives on Criterion Channel This September

The iconic anime series will soon be available for streaming, marking a significant addition to the Criterion Channel's library.

VexlowHQ Staff·July 30, 2026·7 min

Hideaki Anno's acclaimed anime series, Neon Genesis Evangelion, is set to debut on the Criterion Channel in September, inviting both die-hard fans and newcomers to explore its complex narrative and stunning artistry. Originally airing in the mid-1990s, this series has maintained its status as a cornerstone of the anime genre, influencing countless works and earning critical acclaim.

A Landmark in Anime History

Neon Genesis Evangelion, known for its thought-provoking themes and innovative storytelling, diverges from traditional mecha anime. The series not only features giant robots battling monstrous beings known as Angels but also navigates deep psychological terrain, exploring existentialism, depression, and human relationships. Character-driven plots and dense mythology have made Evangelion a subject of analysis in both anime fandom and academic circles.

The series is notable for its rich symbolism and subversion of the tropes common in its genre. It often challenges viewers’ expectations, intertwining feelings of isolation and the question of self-identity amid the chaos of battle. Such themes resonate widely, contributing to its staying power and relevance decades after its initial release.

The Criterion Channel's Expanding Library

The Criterion Channel is known for curating a rich selection of classic and contemporary films, documentaries, and television series that have artistic merit. By adding Neon Genesis Evangelion to its lineup, the service continues to solidify its commitment to showcasing significant cultural works. This partnership represents a notable moment in the evolution of anime's acceptance in wider streaming platforms traditionally dominated by Western media.

The arrival of such an influential series signals a growing acceptance of anime as a legitimate art form within mainstream media. It introduces its audience to the complexities of narration and character that anime can offer, tapping into a broader market of potential viewers.

Engaging a New Generation

For viewers who grew up in the 1990s and the early 2000s, Evangelion remains a pivotal cultural reference point. Its psychological depth and character exploration resonate with the challenges faced by many today. The revival of interest in classic series among a younger generation reflects a larger trend in media consumption where nostalgia meets the need for deeper storytelling in modern entertainment.

Streaming platforms have played a critical role in this revival, bringing older series to a new audience. By incorporating Neon Genesis Evangelion, the Criterion Channel bridges generational gaps, providing both accessibility for veteran fans and an introduction for newcomers.

Future Implications for Anime Streaming

The addition of Neon Genesis Evangelion can have several implications for the future of anime on streaming services. Beyond simply adding content, it showcases an acknowledgment of anime's artistic and narrative diversity. This trend could lead to broader licensing deals for other influential anime titles, potentially reshaping how content is curated on streaming platforms.

Audiences are increasingly demanding diverse content that includes international cinema, and this shift aligns with those interests. It may pave the way for renewed focus on various genres that have been historically overlooked by Western mainstream platforms.

Furthermore, as viewers turn to anime for complex narratives, traditional studios might feel inspired to innovate, potentially leading to fresh storylines and artistic styles in future anime productions.

In anticipation of the series' release in September, fans are encouraged to revisit their favorite moments or dive into the series for the first time and explore the rich narrative set against the backdrop of mechanical battles and philosophical inquiry. As it finds a new home on the Criterion Channel, Neon Genesis Evangelion will likely continue to inspire conversations about its themes and artistry for years to come.
