Cursor Launches ₹649/Month 'Cursor Start' Plan to Grow in India

India is Cursor's third-largest market; the AI coding company is undercutting its own $20 Pro tier with a local plan as its now-completed $60 billion acquisition by SpaceX was moving toward its close.

Leonardo Beltran·July 28, 2026·Updated September 9, 2026·5 min

What Cursor Announced

Cursor, the AI coding assistant made by Anysphere, launched "Cursor Start," a localized subscription priced at ₹649 per month (about $7) for the Indian market -- well below its standard $20/month Pro plan -- giving users access to its Composer 2.5 and Grok 4.5 models with usage limits above the free tier, according to TechCrunch.

Why India

India is Cursor's third-largest market worldwide, with its user base growing more than 200% year over year, per TechCrunch's reporting. The company has hired its first India-based salesperson, is expanding across Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad and Mumbai, and is standing up a government affairs office alongside new technical support roles. Enterprise adoption remains early -- concentrated among individual developers and startups so far -- though Cursor is eyeing banking and larger enterprises next. Simon Green, Cursor's head of Asia-Pacific and Japan, said: "We felt that we had an opportunity there to right-size the commercial model and drive scale."

How This Relates to the SpaceX Deal

Cursor's India push was already underway before the SpaceX deal closed; Green told TechCrunch the expansion plans "were already in motion before the deal." At the time of this announcement (late July 2026), SpaceX's $60 billion all-stock acquisition of Cursor's parent, Anysphere, was still pending; it closed on August 15, 2026 -- see VexlowHQ's separate coverage of the deal's close. Green said SpaceX's existing Starlink presence in India could help accelerate Cursor's local expansion post-acquisition.

Correcting the Record

The previous version of this article described the SpaceX acquisition only vaguely, cited no source, and gave no concrete pricing or market figures. This version adds the confirmed price point, market data, and executive quote from TechCrunch's reporting, and clarifies the acquisition's actual timeline now that it has closed.
