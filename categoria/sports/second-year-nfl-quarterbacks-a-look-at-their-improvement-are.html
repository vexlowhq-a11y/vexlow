Second-Year NFL Quarterbacks: A Look at Their Improvement Areas

As Cam Ward, Jaxson Dart, Tyler Shough, and Shedeur Sanders gear up for their second NFL season, we examine crucial areas where each player must enhance their game to succeed.

Leonardo Beltran·July 30, 2026·7 min

As the NFL gears up for another season, a notable feature will be the progression of four sophomore quarterbacks: Cam Ward, Jaxson Dart, Tyler Shough, and Shedeur Sanders. Entering Year 2, these players have demonstrated potential but still face challenges that need addressing to thrive in a competitive environment. Understanding their individual strengths and weaknesses can provide insight into what fans might expect from them in the upcoming season.

The Development Journey: Year One Reflections

For many quarterbacks, their rookie season serves as a crash course in the complexities of NFL play. The transition from college football, with its various systems and styles, to the NFL's faster pace and more demanding strategies can be overwhelming. This is particularly relevant for Ward, Dart, Shough, and Sanders, each of whom faced different situations and expectations during their first year. The experiences they accumulated on and off the field will likely play a crucial role in shaping their development moving forward.

Ward, for example, had a decent rookie year, but he struggled with consistency in high-pressure situations. Dart, on the other hand, showcased flashes of brilliance but also experienced significant growing pains that limited his effectiveness. Shough, meanwhile, was often scrutinized due to a steep learning curve and a need for improved decision-making. Sanders, heavily touted for his skills, encountered the challenge of adapting to NFL defenses, which often exposed his weaker areas.

Key Improvement Areas for Each Quarterback

The path to improvement isn't identical for each player; their unique styles and experiences dictate different focal points.

  • Cam Ward: His ability to read defenses is essential. Increasing his pre-snap awareness and adjusting to defensive schemes could elevate his game significantly. Developing a quicker release and honing his footwork in the pocket will also be crucial for maximizing his potential.
  • Jaxson Dart: Dart needs to refine his accuracy, particularly on intermediate throws. This aspect is vital for keeping drives alive and minimizing turnovers. Strengthening his connection with receivers through more expansive practice sessions could help him improve in this area.
  • Tyler Shough: For Shough, the primary area for growth is decision-making. In the NFL, the tempo is demanding, and making quick, sound choices is critical. Fostering a better understanding of game situations and optimizing his timing with the ball could help him mature into a more reliable quarterback.
  • Shedeur Sanders: Last year, Sanders was often under pressure, impacting his play. Improving his pocket presence and learning to quickly assess the field under duress will be vital in solidifying his role as a franchise quarterback. Furthermore, enhancing his ability to process the game and anticipate defensive movements can position him for success.

Impact on NFL Quarterback Trends

As these quarterbacks continue to develop, their progression will not only impact their teams but also the broader narrative in the NFL. The league is known for its fierce competition, and growth is often the key to longevity and success in such a challenging environment. These players are not only attempting to improve individually but are also vying for a stronger presence within their teams.

Moreover, their development aligns with a changing perception of quarterback play in the NFL. Teams increasingly favor young quarterbacks with dual-threat capabilities, leading to more pressures both internally and externally for these sophomores to perform. Their subsequent performances will be closely watched not just by fans, but by coaching staff and management, all looking to piece together a winning formula.

The Road Ahead: Success and Challenges

Looking ahead, the 2026 NFL season presents an opportunity for these young quarterbacks to solidify their reputations. Improvement in their respective areas will not only benefit their careers but may significantly influence their team's fortunes. As they enter their second year, fans and analysts alike will be keen observers of their growth trajectories from training camps to regular-season games.

While all four quarterbacks have exhibited potential, the question remains: will they rise to the occasion in 2026? Their commitment to honing their skills, coupled with solid support from their coaching staff and teammates, will be crucial in determining their paths in the NFL. The importance of these formative years cannot be overstated, as they lay the foundation for success in such a demanding league.
