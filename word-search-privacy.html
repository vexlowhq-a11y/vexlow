Word Search — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

This policy covers the Word Search Android app by VexlowHQ (package com.vexlowhq.sopadeletras), separately from the general VexlowHQ website privacy policy.

Information we collect

Word Search doesn't require you to register or create an account to play. We don't collect personal data directly, beyond the standard technical information any app receives (like device type or operating system).

Your game progress (levels completed, stars, achievements, language and volume settings) is stored only on your device and is never sent to our servers. Uninstalling the app, or using the "Reset progress" button in Options, deletes it completely.

Advertising

We use Google AdMob to show ads. AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier, approximate device info, and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads.

  • Rewarded ads: shown only when you choose to watch one, in exchange for a hint — never automatically.
  • Interstitial ads: shown automatically every 10 levels you complete, between levels — not tied to any specific action or reward.
  • Advertising identifiers: used by Google AdMob to serve and, where applicable, personalize ads.

This data is collected and processed by Google directly, per their own privacy policy and ads policy — we don't see or store it ourselves.

Children's privacy

Word Search is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can reset or limit your device's advertising identifier, or opt out of personalized ads, from your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads). You can also delete all local game data at any time from Options → Reset progress, or by uninstalling the app.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We'll post any significant changes on this same page.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, email us at contact@vexlowhq.com.
